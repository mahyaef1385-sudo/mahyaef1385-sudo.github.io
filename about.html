Hi, I’m Mahya.

I’m a UX designer with a background in architecture, Montessori education and user-centered design. I love creating calm, intuitive digital experiences that make people’s everyday lives easier.

My journey started in architecture, where I learned to think in systems, shapes and human movement. Later, as a Montessori teacher, I saw how deeply design affects behavior, emotions and confidence. Today I bring both worlds into my UX practice: clear structure, warm simplicity and purposeful problem solving.

I’m passionate about designing products that support real people, especially busy women balancing work, family and life. I believe good design should feel effortless: less noise, more clarity, and more moments of “Oh, that makes sense.”

When I’m not designing, you can find me exploring fashion, organizing closets, painting, or creating beautiful spaces for children.
