How I work

  • Research first

    I start with interviews and observation, not assumptions. Years of teaching in Montessori classrooms taught me to notice what people do, not just what they say, and architecture taught me to think about how people move through a space.

  • Simple and clear

    I turn complicated flows into clear steps and plain labels, like swapping investing jargon for words teens actually use. Less noise, more clarity, and designs that make everyday life easier.

  • End to end

    From research and wireframes to usability testing and handoff, I carry the work through. Every project here was tested with the people it was made for.
